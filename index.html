03 / THE RESEARCH DIRECTIONS

Build boldly.
Verify relentlessly.

Four places where artificial imagination meets experiments. These are research directions, not claims of completed studies.

NO FICTIONAL BREAKTHROUGHS. NO INVENTED PERFORMANCE NUMBERS.
FIELD NOTE / 01

Distributed cognition

Join the complementary strengths of human judgment, local models, external tools, and durable memory.

Example investigation +

Compare a human-only solution with a human–AI–tool workflow on the same design challenge. Track errors, time, and independently judged usefulness.

FIELD NOTE / 02

Living worlds

Model ecosystems whose visual forms and behaviors follow coherent physical and biological causes.

Example investigation +

Test whether an interactive forest responds consistently to seasons, water supply, terrain, and species interaction rather than merely looking plausible.

FIELD NOTE / 03

Embodied intelligence

Move from words into sensors, simulations, robots, and feedback grounded in the physical world.

Example investigation +

Quantify map drift under sensor noise in a controlled virtual robot, then compare errors against a measured ground truth.

FIELD NOTE / 04

Verification & governance

Build systems that can show what they did, what failed, and precisely when a human must intervene.

Example investigation +

Run a bounded local agent against an allowlisted task set. Test STOP latency, receipt integrity, and denial of forbidden actions.

04 / THE HUMAN AUTHORITY CHARTER

Imagination without accountability is just spectacle.

The consortium is a proposal for collaborative research, not a legally established organization or evidence of autonomous machine consciousness. The machine can propose; the human decides.
