# ORION Artificial Imagination Consortium

An independent, human-governed research concept exploring distributed cognition, artificial imagination, living worlds, embodied intelligence, and verifiable experimentation.

The web experience includes a clearly labeled **illustrative** thought-cycle demo and a browser-only experiment notebook. It does not connect to any personal devices, local AI service, private tools, or real-world machinery.

The founding design quote and addendum are attributed to Justin / Professor M (November 8, 2026). No legal incorporation or third-party affiliation is represented.

Open index.html to run. No build tools or credentials required. Experiment drafts are saved only in your browser; export them to keep a backup.

Design principle: Word → Form → Refinement → Function → Purpose → Word.
